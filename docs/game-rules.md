# Game Rules

The application delegates chess rules to chess.js 0.10.3. This keeps rule handling centralized and avoids custom rule bugs.

## Supported Rules

- Legal pawn movement.
- First pawn double move.
- Pawn captures.
- En passant.
- Queen promotion.
- Rook, knight, bishop, queen, and king movement.
- Castling on both sides when legal.
- Check detection.
- Checkmate detection.
- Stalemate and draw detection.
- Illegal move prevention.
- Turn switching.

## Promotion

Pawn promotion automatically promotes to a queen. This preserves the original app behavior and keeps the drag/drop flow simple.

## Human And AI Sides

- The human plays White.
- The AI plays Black.
- White pieces can only be dragged on White's turn.
- Black moves are generated by the AI after a legal White move or when the AI Move button is pressed on Black's turn.

## Board Synchronization

After every drop and snap animation, the chessboard.js position is reset from `game.fen()`. This guarantees special moves such as castling, en passant, and promotion display correctly.
